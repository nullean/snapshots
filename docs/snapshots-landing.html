Small, strict, readable

A snapshot test is only as good as the moment you look at its failure. Everything here is built for that moment.

plain text
Messages people and agents can act on

No colour codes, repository-relative paths, at most 20 differences and 80 lines, and the exact command to accept.
structural
Compared as DOMs and trees

Attribute order, whitespace, key order and 1 versus 1.0 do not fail a test. A changed field is reported by its path.
never automatic
You accept, or nothing changes

No auto-accept, ever. SNAPSHOT_ACCEPT=1 works on your machine; in CI it is ignored and the failure says so.
tidy
One folder per project

Verified files under .snapshots/, mirroring your tests. Received files in obj/ or your artifacts folder, never in source.
native AOT
A CLI that starts instantly

snap reviews, accepts, rejects and finds orphaned snapshots, native on Linux, macOS and Windows.
lightweight
Trim- and AOT-safe throughout

No heavy core, and every write is scoped to the snapshot folders, so even a bug cannot touch anything else.

Four formats

Each stored as UTF-8 with LF endings, each compared its own way.

FormatAssertionsCompared
TextMatchSnapshotline by line, shown as unified hunks
HTMLMatchHtmlSnapshot, BeHtml, ContainHtmlas a DOM with AngleSharp.Diffing; select, remove and transform first
JSONMatchJsonSnapshot, BeJsonas a tree: keys in any order, numbers by value, arrays aligned item by item
YAMLMatchYamlSnapshot, BeYamlas a tree with the YAML 1.2 core schema, stored as written, comments and all

Review, then accept

snap walks through what changed, one snapshot at a time. Accept it, reject it, or skip it for later. Keys can be given in full, as a unique suffix, or as a glob.
